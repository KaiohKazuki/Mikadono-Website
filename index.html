Introduction

Dealing with Mikadono Sisters Is a Breeze is a romantic comedy anime series based on the manga by Aya Hirakawa. The story follows an ordinary orphan named Yū Ayase who moves in with three brilliant, high-achieving prodigy sisters known as the "Three Royals," blending heartfelt domestic life with romantic comedy hijinks as they slowly warm up to him.

History

Aya Hirakawa the author of the anime started writing and illustrating the original Dealing with Mikadono Sisters Is a Breeze manga series in December 2021 Produced by the animation studio P.A. Works, the television series premiered in Japan on July 9, 2025, with international streaming following on Crunchyroll.

Author

Aya Hirakawa is a Japanese manga artist best known as the creator, writer, and illustrator of the romantic comedy manga series Dealing with Mikadono Sisters Is a Breeze.

Anime Recap

Dealing with Mikadono Sisters Is a Breeze is not a feature film, but rather an anime television series (and manga) that follows Yū Ayase, an exceptionally ordinary and grounded orphan who moves in with a wealthy family after the passing of his superstar mother. He finds himself living with three brilliant, high-achieving sisters known at their elite school as the "Three Royals"—each a prodigy in a different field (academics, athletics, and strategy games). Despite their initial coldness and perfectionist pressure, Yū wins them over with his domestic skills, emotional resilience, and care, gradually melting their defenses and sparking romantic comedy hijinks as the gifted sisters all begin falling for him.
